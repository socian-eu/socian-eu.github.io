Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Glowy ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by SOCIAN SOFT S.R.L., collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

a. Information Automatically Collected

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain technical diagnostic data through trusted third-party services to ensure app stability and performance:

  • Usage Data: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous aggregated analytics on app interactions (e.g., screen views, button clicks) to understand which features are helpful.
  • Crash Reports: We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymized stack traces and crash logs when unexpected errors occur so we can rapidly resolve them.
  • Device Identifiers: We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) which may utilize advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or AAID on Android) in accordance with your system tracking permissions to serve advertisements.
  • Subscription Data: We use RevenueCat to manage, validate, and track your in-app purchase and subscription entitlements. We never receive or store your payment card numbers; all payments are handled securely by Apple via the App Store.

b. Information You Provide & Local-First Architecture

The App is intentionally architected to be 100% local-first without requiring any user registration or account creation:

  • Your mood check-in records, personal reflections, saved affirmations, custom categories, and streak tracking are stored locally on your device storage.
  • We do not store your personal identity, name, or email on remote databases.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's features and user experience.
  • Diagnose technical bugs and crashes to ensure app stability.
  • Process and verify subscription entitlements via Apple App Store and RevenueCat.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers strictly necessary to operate the App:

  • Google (Firebase & AdMob): For anonymized usage analytics, crash reporting, and optional ad serving.
  • RevenueCat: For secure in-app subscription and receipt validation.
  • Apple App Store: For billing, payment processing, and application distribution.

4. Your Rights & Data Control

Because your affirmations, mood logs, and preferences are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can erase this data at any time simply by clearing app data or uninstalling the App. For any questions regarding your data rights under GDPR or other privacy regulations, please contact us.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

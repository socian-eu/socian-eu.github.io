Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions regarding Glowy, subscriptions, and widgets.

How do daily affirmations and mood check-ins work?

Glowy is built with a 100% local-first and privacy-focused architecture.

  • No login or sign-up: You never need to create an account, password, or provide personal credentials.
  • Device-only storage: Your daily affirmations, liked quotes, customized themes, personal notes, and mood check-in records are saved directly in your device's local storage.
  • Your privacy comes first: We do not track personal identifiers, nor do we sell or monetize your personal emotional reflections.
How do subscriptions, free trials, and cancellations work?

Glowy offers both a free tier and an optional Glowy Pro subscription (Monthly and Annual plans).

  • 3-Day Free Trial: The Annual plan comes with a 3-day free trial, giving you full access to unlimited affirmations, all theme packs, AI-assisted affirmations, and all widget designs.
  • Billing & Renewal: Payments are processed directly and securely by Apple via your Apple ID. Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours prior to the end of the billing period.
  • How to Cancel or Manage: You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time directly through iOS:
    1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
    2. Tap your Name / Apple ID at the top.
    3. Tap Subscriptions.
    4. Select Glowy and choose Cancel Subscription.
How do I restore my purchase on a new or existing device?

If you got a new iPhone, restored your device from backup, or reinstalled Glowy:

  1. Open the Glowy app.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear icon) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Restore Purchases".

Glowy will communicate securely with the App Store to re-activate your Glowy Pro subscription immediately. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the initial purchase.

How do I add Glowy widgets to my iOS Home Screen?

Glowy includes Small and Medium Home Screen widgets so you can view uplifting thoughts without even opening the app:

  1. From the Home Screen, touch and hold any empty area or app icon until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap the + (Add) button in the upper-left corner.
  3. Scroll down or type "Glowy" in the search bar.
  4. Swipe to select either the Small or Medium widget format.
  5. Tap Add Widget, then tap Done.

Tip: Widgets refresh automatically throughout the day with your favorite categories!

How do I request a refund?

Because Apple handles all App Store billing and payment transactions directly, developers cannot issue refunds on Apple's behalf.

To request a refund from Apple:

  1. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID and password.
  3. Select "I'd like to..." → "Request a refund".
  4. Choose the reason and select Glowy from your purchased items.
